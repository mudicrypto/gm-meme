Questions.

Does Sift tell me what to buy?

No. Sift shows facts and red flags. It never tells you to buy or sell, and nothing in it is financial advice.

Can Sift access my wallet?

Not your phone wallet or Phantom: those keys never leave your device or wallet app. The optional Sift wallet's keys are held by our wallet infrastructure provider, with limits and a freeze switch you control.

Can Sift recover a lost phone wallet?

No. Only you have the key, so back it up in the app. That's also why nobody else can move your funds.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Friends → Delete account. It removes everything Sift keeps about you.
